# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Simulark serves a mixed audience united by one job: turning ideas into credible backend architecture before code ships.

- **Solo and full-stack engineers** sketching services, data flows, and integrations quickly
- **Tech leads and staff engineers** aligning teams on system design and trade-offs
- **Founders and PMs** translating product requirements into diagrams they can discuss with engineering
- **Anyone learning system design** who needs a visual, editable starting point rather than a blank canvas

Users arrive with a problem to model (greenfield app, refactor, scale-up, integration) and expect the tool to meet them where they are — prompt, plan document, or existing canvas.

## Product Purpose

Simulark is an AI-assisted backend architecture design platform. Plain-English descriptions (and optional PRD/plan uploads) become interactive XYFlow diagrams that users can edit, validate, stress-test, and export.

Success looks like three outcomes in the first session:

1. **Speed** — prompt to a credible diagram in under a minute (Flash tier for iteration)
2. **Trust** — validation, tech normalization, quality scoring, and exports a team can actually use (Pro tier for depth)
3. **Iteration** — chat + canvas as a creative workspace, not a one-shot generator

The canvas is the source of truth. The model proposes structured output; deterministic code enforces rules, normalizes tech choices, and renders the graph.

## Brand Personality

**Playful builder** — creative tool energy, not corporate SaaS.

- Feels like a workshop for people who enjoy building systems, not a compliance dashboard
- Terminal / Mission Control flavor (monospace accents, operator language) without cosplay seriousness
- Confident and capable, with room for delight in motion and micro-interactions
- Shows craft on marketing pages; stays out of the way in the product shell where work happens

## Anti-references

Explicitly avoid **obvious AI slop**:

- Gradient text, decorative glassmorphism, purple-on-white defaults
- Tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section
- Identical icon + heading + blurb card grids repeated endlessly
- Hero-metric templates and numbered section scaffolding (01 · 02 · 03) without real sequence meaning
- Generic "AI made this" polish that could belong to any workflow tool

Marketing should feel designed for Simulark, not templated from category defaults.

## Design Principles

1. **Build, don't pitch** — The product proves itself through the canvas and exports; marketing shows real architecture outcomes, not abstract promises.
2. **Speed with guardrails** — Fast generation is worthless without validation and normalization users can trust.
3. **Canvas-first** — Every AI interaction serves the graph; chat is a control surface, not the destination.
4. **Playful precision** — Builder energy in voice and motion; precision in typography, spacing, and technical copy.
5. **Split register discipline** — Product UI optimizes for focus and workflow; marketing pages earn brand-level craft without polluting the editor chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices as baseline: semantic HTML, keyboard reachability for interactive controls, sufficient color contrast on body text, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for animations.

No special compliance tier beyond WCAG-minded defaults; revisit if enterprise customers require formal AA/AAA certification.
